Tired of emptying your bag chest by chest?
One click puts your items into the chests around you that already hold the same items.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]One-click sorting[/b] from the inventory screen (Character tab), with mouse or controller[/*]
[*][b]Items go where they belong:[/b] into chests that already contain that item, or into storages made for it (like firewood
sheds)[/*]
[*][b]Keeps what you need:[/b] tools, hotbar items, the seed in your hand, faith, bags and quest items stay with you[/*]
[*][b]Sort a bag:[/b] open a bag and sort, and only that bag's contents go into the chests[/*]
[*][b]Works like the game:[/b] the button looks and behaves like the game's own move-all button, and items are moved the same way the
chest window moves them[/*]
[*][b]Translated[/b] into all the game's languages[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to use[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Mouse:[/b] click the button on the right of the Inventory header (with a bag open: on the bag's header)[/*]
[*][b]Controller:[/b] press [b]LT[/b] on the Character page; the prompt is shown at the end of the tip bar[/*]
[/list]
The button turns grey when there is nothing to sort or no chest nearby.
With a bag open, only that bag is sorted. Otherwise only your inventory is sorted and bags keep their contents.

[size=4][b]What gets sorted[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Inside a storage area (home, kitchen, yard...): that area's storages, the same ones the inventory screen lists[/*]
[*]Outside an area: storages within 10 units (adjustable)[/*]
[*]Items go to chests that already contain them (the one holding the most first), then to storages made for them[/*]
[*]Items no nearby chest contains yet stay with you, and so does whatever doesn't fit. Turn on [i]OverflowToOtherChests[/i] to put them
into any nearby chest with room[/*]
[*]Conveyor chests and pallets are skipped by default, because production lines take items from them[/*]
[*]Unstackable items keep their durability and bag contents[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]

[list]

[/list]

[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Download the file, open the zip and copy its [i]BepInEx[/i] folder into the game folder (Steam Library → right-click Graveyard Keeper 2
→ Manage → Browse local files); merge the folders.

Game folder after installing:
[code]
Graveyard Keeper 2
└─ BepInEx
    └─ plugins
        └─ GK2SortToNearbyChests
            └─ GK2SortToNearbyChests.dll   ← new[/code]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][i]BepInEx\config\gk2.sorttonearbychests.cfg[/i] is created on first start[/*]
[*]Choose what stays in your inventory, the search radius, overflow and conveyor use[/*]
[*]Changes apply after restarting the game[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]BepInEx\plugins\GK2SortToNearbyChests[/i].
